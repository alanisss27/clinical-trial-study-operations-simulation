## Approved aggregate assessment rules

Apply the highest supported material condition without averaging; a critical material condition takes precedence over numerous minor On Track items.

| Overall health | Evidence rule |
|---|---|
| Blocked | An unmet prerequisite or unresolved issue currently prevents required work and materially obstructs study execution. |
| At Risk | No material blockage exists, but evidence threatens a material milestone, required dependency or deliverable and recovery is not sufficiently supported. |
| On Track | Available evidence supports current material delivery forecasts and neither At Risk nor Blocked conditions apply. |
| Not assessable | Missing evidence prevents a defensible assessment of material delivery conditions. A known material threat or blockage still takes precedence. |

| Evidence confidence | Evidence rule |
|---|---|
| Sufficient | Evidence supports assessment of current material delivery conditions. |
| Limited | Assessment is possible, but identified evidence gaps could materially change it. |
| Insufficient | Evidence gaps prevent a defensible assessment; health is Not assessable unless a material threat or blockage is already established. |

Milestone health is evidence, not an automatic roll-up. Baseline variance contributes only when it materially threatens current execution. Active issues contribute through demonstrated effects. Prospective risks, including High risks, do not automatically deteriorate overall health. Future dependencies and unverified future execution assumptions do not automatically reduce confidence when current conditions remain defensibly assessable. Missing evidence must not hide a known threat or blockage. Aggregate ratings remain distinct from risk priority, issue status, deliverable delivery/acceptance and individual milestone health; all existing escalation rules remain independent.
