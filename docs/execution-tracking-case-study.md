**Step 9 complete - SIM-001.** Independent fictional PM simulation. This case demonstrates evidence-based disposition; it does not require every action or exception to be closed. All execution evidence below is PM-approved fictional evidence, not real study records or professional experience.

## Path boundaries and PM role

**Scenario B is canonical**, progressing through the Apr 23, 2027 pickup checkpoint. **Scenario C is an alternative fictional exercise only** and changes no canonical records. Evidence from the paths is never combined. The [Apr 12 weekly report](weekly-status-report.md) remains unchanged as a historical snapshot; no intervening reports or meetings are reconstructed.

The PM integrates functional evidence, compares actuals against plan, evaluates dependencies and tracks disposition. Site/Investigator/clinical functions own participant scheduling, staffing and readiness. Logistics owns dispatch execution under its existing arrangement; the PM does not perform or assume those functions.

## B. Scenario C - alternative fictional execution branch only

### Plan, missed execution and supported reforecast

The alternative retains Apr 12 enrollment and Apr 16 readiness, but substitutes a missed Apr 19 administration because the participant was unavailable. Initially no replacement date was confirmed; no actual or unsupported forecast was recorded. Functional owners then supplied availability, clinical applicability/readiness and operational confirmation supporting **Apr 21 as the revised forecast**. Exact receipt timestamps beyond the approved exercise evidence are not invented.

Scenario-only minimum clinical assumptions: administration may occur 7-10 calendar days after enrollment subject to readiness; missing a planned date neither cancels enrollment nor authorizes later dosing; participant-specific eligibility/medical readiness must be reconfirmed; Sponsor Medical confirms timing applicability and the Investigator confirms permission to proceed. No downstream visit flexibility is created. Disposition/replacement rules remain Not established; this is not a full protocol and does not amend the canonical seven-day convention.

Site/clinical functions confirm administration actually occurred Apr 21 with applicable requirements and readiness satisfied. Actual versus original Apr 19 plan is **+2 calendar days**; actual versus supported Apr 21 reforecast is zero. No material administration exception or immediate disruption to other activities was reported. Future visit dates below are derived plans, not completed visits or automatic proof of capacity.

## Controls on progression and closure

Milestone completion, action-component completion, exception disposition and risk closure are different decisions. Advancing a date does not prove an event. An effective control for one event is not a broad effectiveness claim. Unresolved confirmation evidence remains visible even when no health change is justified.

Established governance remains unchanged: functional owners act within authority; the Lead integrates unresolved cross-functional matters; Sponsor involvement follows established authority and triggers. A prerequisite forecast to miss its due date and move a dependent milestone is escalated within one business day rather than waiting for reporting. This case creates no new authority, clinical requirements outside the alternative assumptions, or additional milestone-health rules.

The canonical record progresses only through supplied evidence. The Apr 12 report remains historical; this case is the dated execution update, not a reconstructed weekly report. Step 10 scope is not established or started.
