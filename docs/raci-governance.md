## Roles and interpretation

- **Sponsor:** Avenrix Study Sponsor; retains study oversight, funding, vendor-agreement and material schedule/scope approval authority, and site-activation authorization.
- **Lead:** Sponsor Clinical Project Manager / Study Lead; integrates the schedule, coordinates dependencies and acceptance, and prepares escalation and decision requests.
- **CRO:** Brindlepath Clinical Support Lead and relevant startup, monitoring, or closeout staff. Coordinates readiness evidence and recommends activation; cannot authorize activation.
- **Sites:** Applicable site teams; **Investigator** means the investigator accountable for that site's clinical decisions and participant safety. Grouped site rows apply separately at each site, not to a newly appointed cross-site authority.
- **Lab:** Orivelle Central Laboratory; **Lab Quality** is its existing quality-release function.
- **Logistics:** Parcelgrove Clinical Logistics, under the lab's service arrangement.
- **DM:** Dedicated Brindlepath data-management function; separate from CRO coordination in this matrix.
- **Stats:** Sponsor-appointed biostatistics function.
- **Safety:** Sponsor safety/pharmacovigilance function. **Medical:** Established sponsor medical oversight; distinguished from project staff and from safety-process ownership.

**R** performs or coordinates the specified work. **A** is accountable for that row's defined output within existing authority. **C** provides required input before the output or decision. **I** receives relevant status or outcomes. A role may be both R and A; roles listed under A are not merely consulted. Functional accountability does not remove Sponsor oversight. A dash means no standing assignment for that row, not exclusion from relevant communications.

The matrix is always applicable, not activated only by risk or delay. C/I assignments describe work interfaces, not standing meeting attendance. Separate rows distinguish functional delivery from acceptance, quality release, clinical progression, and required sign-offs. No RACI label permits an owner to waive another function's prerequisite or approval.

## Weekly coordination and attendance

The Lead coordinates the established weekly cross-functional meeting. The **standing core is Lead + CRO Lead only**. DM, Sites, Sponsor decision owners and other functional representatives attend on an agenda basis when their dependencies, risks, issues, decisions or upcoming milestones require participation.

| Agenda trigger | Required relevant representation |
|---|---|
| Material data issues, lab transfers, reconciliation, queries or database-lock dependencies | DM; Lab, Safety, Stats and other signatories as relevant |
| Site readiness, capacity, participant/site operational issues or site-dependent milestones | Affected site representative; Investigator when clinical input or authority is needed |
| Lab package, quality release, specimen instructions or lab-data readiness | Lab; Lab Quality when release authority is needed |
| Pickup, shipment tracking or delivery dependencies | Logistics; affected Lab/Sites |
| Safety reconciliation or medical/clinical progression input | Safety and/or Medical; relevant Investigator according to retained authority |
| Analysis requirements, lock readiness or statistical summary | Stats; DM |
| Funding, vendor agreements, material schedule/scope decisions, activation authorization or unresolved escalations | Sponsor decision owner; affected functional owners |

Participation is driven by the work, not by whether a milestone is already At Risk. An On Track milestone may still require functional attendance or approval. RACI assignments persist between meetings.

If a required function cannot attend, obtain sufficient written input/approval where appropriate, or participation by an appropriately authorized delegate. **Absence is never consent.** Delegation does not transfer or expand established approval authority. If sufficient input or authority remains unavailable, **defer only the affected decision**; unrelated meeting business continues. A written opinion is not an approval unless supplied by the required authority for that decision.

## Resolution, escalation and recovery

1. Functional owners first attempt resolution within their existing responsibilities and authority.
2. If owner-level resolution fails, the Lead coordinates cross-functional resolution.
3. Escalate to the Sponsor when resolution still cannot be reached or Sponsor-level authority is required.
4. Do not wait for these stages or the weekly meeting when the established schedule trigger is met: **a prerequisite forecast to miss its due date and move a dependent milestone is escalated to the Sponsor decision owner within one business day**.

The Lead prepares the impact, dependency, options and decision request; Sponsor-level resolution does not override clinical, safety or quality prerequisites. The established lab issue uses daily recovery checks until the blocking deliverable is accepted.
