## Reading the tracker

All dates are 2027. PM planning uses Monday-Friday without holiday exclusions; elapsed working-day durations begin after predecessor completion. Planning targets are not contractual commitments. Approved fictional assumptions are not protocol requirements, validated Lab estimates, SLAs, staffing commitments or execution evidence.

Delivery means the item was submitted/provided. Acceptance separately requires established criteria, authority and evidence. Neither delivery alone nor acceptance alone completes a milestone: all milestone conditions still apply. Supporting work is not automatically a separate vendor deliverable.

Delivery status may be Not yet delivered, Delivered, or Not evidenced where available records do not establish actual delivery. Acceptance may be Pending, Rework required, Accepted, or **Not established**. Not established means criteria and/or authority are missing; it is not rejection, rework or noncompliance. No rework is asserted without evidence. All future planned items have no actual delivery/acceptance evidence in this snapshot.
